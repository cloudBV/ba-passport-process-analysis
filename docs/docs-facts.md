# Facts: What the Sources Say

Source: SOP-INOVASI (see SOURCES.md). All facts are in my own words. Page numbers are
PDF pages. Interpretation lives in pain-points.md. Anything I could not read or confirm
is under "Open questions".

## Open questions (not facts)
- Is the MILES written request on paper or electronic? The SOP says "written" only (p.6).
- The slide shows the applicant submitting collective files to the Loket (p.9, step 4), while the text says applicants register through M-Paspor (p.6). Are these the same step?
- The school row's processing time is blank in the PDF text (p.6).
- The meaning of the slide box "Tidak tersedia layanan EAZY Paspor" is unclear (p.9).
- Can officers see M-Paspor registrations before the MILES date? Not stated.

## Not in the sources
Volumes, costs beyond the fee, error rates, rejected-document handling for MILES, payment failures, tracking after dispatch to the post office.
