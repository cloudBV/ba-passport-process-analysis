## Limitations
- Some text extracted from the PDF is garbled (notably pp.6-8), and the slides are low resolution.
- The SOPs describe the intended process, not observed practice. Never confirmed with staff.
- The SOP contradicts itself in places (see P5 in pain-points.md). Where it does, both versions are recorded.
- No volumes, times beyond the stated standards, costs beyond the fee, or error rates.
- Any pain point beyond what the document shows is labeled "Inferred".
